Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Forever Begins (operated by GridWise Solutions) collects when you use this website, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide when registering as a couple or vendor (name, email, phone, business details).
  • Booking & inquiry details you submit through booking forms, appointment requests and contact forms, including preferences like guest count, budget, dates and event details.
  • Content you upload, such as profile photos, portfolio images, videos and reviews.
  • Usage data such as pages viewed and vendor profile views, used to show vendors basic analytics about their own listing.

2. How We Use It

  • To connect couples with vendors and enable booking and appointment requests.
  • To display vendor profiles, packages and reviews to prospective couples.
  • To respond to support and contact form messages.
  • To improve the site and prevent misuse.

3. How Data Is Stored

This site stores account, booking and profile data in your browser's local storage on the device you use. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing With Vendors

When you submit a booking or appointment request, the details you provide (name, contact info, event preferences) are shared with the specific vendor you are booking with, so they can respond to your request.

5. Your Choices

You may update or remove your profile information at any time from your account dashboard. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

6. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent via the Contact Us section or WhatsApp at +961 76 346 074.
